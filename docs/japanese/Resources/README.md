## 1. Struktur Belajar yang Direkomendasikan

### Level Pemula (N5-N4)
**Durasi**: 3-6 bulan
- Hiragana & Katakana (2 minggu)
- Grammar dasar & partikel (1 bulan)
- Kosakata harian (500 kata)
- Kanji dasar (100 kanji)

### Level Menengah (N3)
**Durasi**: 6-12 bulan
- Grammar intermediate
- Keigo dasar
- Kosakata bisnis (1000+ kata)
- Kanji (300-500 kanji)
- Listening practice

### Level Lanjut (N2-N1)
**Durasi**: 1-2 tahun
- Keigo advanced
- Technical vocabulary
- Business communication
- Kanji (1000+ kanji)
- Native-level comprehension

## 6. Practice Methods

### Daily Routine (1-2 jam/hari)

**Pagi (30 menit)**
- [ ] Review Anki flashcards
- [ ] Listen to Japanese podcast while commuting

**Siang (30 menit)**
- [ ] Read 1 NHK News Easy article
- [ ] Learn 5 new vocabulary words

**Malam (30-60 menit)**
- [ ] Grammar study (textbook/video)
- [ ] Writing practice (diary/email)
- [ ] Kanji practice

### Weekly Goals
- [ ] Learn 30-50 new words
- [ ] Master 2-3 grammar patterns
- [ ] Write 3-5 journal entries
- [ ] Have 1 conversation session
- [ ] Watch 2-3 Japanese videos

### Monthly Checkpoints
- [ ] Take practice JLPT test
- [ ] Review all learned grammar
- [ ] Write longer composition (500+ characters)
- [ ] Record speaking progress

## 8. Testing & Certification

### JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test)
| Level | Kanji | Vocabulary | Study Hours |
|-------|-------|------------|-------------|
| N5 | ~100 | ~800 | 300-400 hrs |
| N4 | ~300 | ~1500 | 600-700 hrs |
| N3 | ~650 | ~3750 | 900-1100 hrs |
| N2 | ~1000 | ~6000 | 1500-2000 hrs |
| N1 | ~2000 | ~10000 | 3000-4500 hrs |

**Test Dates**: July & December
**Registration**: April-May (July), August-September (December)

### JFT-Basic
- Computer-based test
- Available more frequently
- Focus on practical communication

### BJT (Business Japanese Proficiency Test)
- Business-focused
- Scoring: 0-800 points
- Preferred by employers

## 9. Common Mistakes & Solutions

### Mistake 1: Overusing English Word Order
**Problem**: Direct translation from English
**Solution**: Practice SOV structure daily

### Mistake 2: Confusing Particles
**Problem**: は vs が，に vs で
**Solution**: Create example sentences for each

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Keigo
**Problem**: Using casual form in business
**Solution**: Memorize essential keigo verbs early

### Mistake 4: Not Practicing Output
**Problem**: Only reading/listening
**Solution**: Write diary, speak with partners

### Mistake 5: Giving Up on Kanji
**Problem**: Kanji seems overwhelming
**Solution**: Use mnemonics, learn radicals first

## 12. Quick Start Guide (30 Hari Pertama)

### Minggu 1: Hiragana & Katakana
- Hari 1-3: Hiragana dasar
- Hari 4-5: Hiragana dengan dakuten
- Hari 6-7: Katakana dasar

### Minggu 2: Basic Grammar
- Partikel: は，が，を，に
- Verb conjugation: masu-form
- Simple sentences

### Minggu 3: Expanding
- i-adjectives & na-adjectives
- Negative & past forms
- Basic vocabulary (100 words)

### Minggu 4: Practice
- Simple conversations
- Reading short texts
- Writing diary entries

**Setelah 30 hari**: Kamu bisa membaca hiragana/katakana, membuat kalimat sederhana, dan kosakata dasar!
