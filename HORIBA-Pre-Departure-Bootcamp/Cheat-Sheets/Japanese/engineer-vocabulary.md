## Tips for Learning Japanese

1. **Start with greetings** - Learn basic polite expressions first
2. **Practice daily** - Even 15 minutes a day helps
3. **Use flashcards** - Apps like Anki or Duolingo
4. **Watch technical videos** - Japanese engineering YouTube channels
5. **Practice with colleagues** - Don't be afraid to make mistakes
6. **Learn Kanji gradually** - Focus on common technical characters first
7. **Understand honorifics** - Important for workplace communication
